# Xi'an Breaking Mo Table Figure Prompt

Use case: realistic-editorial-miniature

Asset type: full-width figure for the B6 multilingual travel book and its
synchronized responsive website.

Continuity references: use the four hash-pinned guide-character files and the
three recurring travel-tool files recorded in `data/sources/catalog.json`.
They remain read-only outside this repository. Aya-chan, Lala Xia, Sasa-kun,
and the Zhuangzi robot are four equal traveling companions shared with the
LALACHAN travel-video series. The robot is a person in the scene, not equipment.

Primary request: create a brand-new high-resolution landscape editorial travel
photograph of the table action that begins a conventional beef or lamb paomo
meal in Xi'an. Render the four guide designs as convincing tactile collectible
figures in a real restaurant setting, with realistic materials, grounded
shadows, natural warm daylight, and restrained depth of field. Preserve the
red-panda sailor design of Aya-chan, Lala Xia's round panda form and pale
overalls, Sasa-kun's panda hoodie, and the Zhuangzi robot's compact white-and-
black articulated body and single camera-eye head. Do not turn them into human
actors or generic mascots.

Teaching action: Aya-chan, Lala Xia, and Sasa-kun tear a firm, dense, flat round
wheat mo into small uneven pieces over one broad white ceramic bowl. The
Zhuangzi robot participates with one articulated hand holding a piece and the
other steadying the bowl. The bowl contains only dry bread pieces: no broth,
noodles, meat, or garnish yet. Show one intact dense mo and one partly torn mo
on a side plate so the sequence remains clear at about 9 cm print width.

Supporting food: include only small side dishes of whole peeled garlic and red
chilli paste. Bread should have a compact crumb and restrained toasted spots,
not resemble pita, naan, a fluffy bun, tortilla, or sliced Western bread.

Recurring tools: place the patchwork travel notebook, folded black LightMind
glasses, and a small e-paper word-card device together at the far edge of the
table. Keep them quiet and secondary. The device should show only abstract
colored shapes or a blank screen; do not reproduce source-screen words, logos,
or pseudo-writing.

Composition: horizontal 3:2, medium-wide overhead three-quarter angle. Keep all
four guides visible from the torso or waist up without cropped heads. The bowl
and tearing action must dominate the central field. Use plausible paws, hands,
and robot articulation; no duplicate character, extra person, impossible grip,
or awkwardly cut joint.

Palette and mood: vivid but natural vermilion, jade, cobalt, and coral accents
against warm wood and white ceramics. The result should read as careful travel-
magazine photography, not a toy advertisement, fantasy tableau, restaurant
promotion, or product display.

Constraints: no restaurant identity, product logo, caption, signage, watermark,
legible text, historic costume, ceremonial pose, famous landmark, alcohol,
pork, finished soup, machine-cut cubes, knives, luxury tableware, dark orange
grading, excessive steam, or clutter that competes with the procedure.
