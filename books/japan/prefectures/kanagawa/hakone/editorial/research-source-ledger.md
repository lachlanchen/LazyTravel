# Hakone Research Source Ledger

Ledger opened: `2026-08-16`. Destination gate:
`japan/prefectures/kanagawa/hakone`.

This ledger defines the factual base for the Hakone book. It is not draft
prose. Official and institutional sources establish durable claims; current
operators establish volatile travel details. Commercial tourism pages may
help identify a question, but they do not outrank a municipality, museum,
transport operator, shrine, geopark, or national agency for facts within that
institution's remit.

No external guide PDF, source archive, or website snapshot is copied into this
repository. URLs and check dates are recorded here. Derived maps, notes,
aligned JSON, reviewed text, and approved project-owned figures are the only
publishable artifacts.

## Rejected or Deferred Claims

| Claim | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| "The Hakone loop is the correct way to see Hakone." | Rejected. It is one transport pattern; direction, overnight district, weather, crowds, and onward journey change the useful route. |
| "Mount Fuji is visible from Lake Ashi or the ropeway." | Rejected as a promise. Describe possible viewpoints and give a cloud/fog fallback. |
| "Eating one black egg adds seven years to your life." | Retain only as clearly labeled local legend. Never place it in a health or practical-advice block. |
| "Hakone Shrine was founded in a precise ancient year." | Deferred unless the sentence is explicitly attributed to shrine tradition and separated from historical evidence. |
| "All Hakone onsen are tattoo-friendly" or "tattoos are forbidden." | Rejected. Rules vary by property and may change. |
| "A named hotel is the best place to stay." | Rejected. Compare districts and traveler needs first; named properties require current, transparent criteria. |
| Exact fares, pass value, hours, seasonal dates, last departures, road closures, ropeway operation, and trail availability. | Deferred to dated practical blocks and final preflight checks. |
| Generic soba, kaiseki, curry, or cafe food as uniquely Hakone cuisine. | Rejected without a specific local history or place-based reason. |

## Volatile-Data Rule

Every operational block must carry `checked_at`, `source_ids`, and a short
recheck instruction. The release build must fail when a time-sensitive block
lacks those fields. Final verification covers:

- live transport and ropeway status;
- current pass coverage and price;
- museum, checkpoint, and shrine visitor information;
- volcanic alert, gas, road, and trail restrictions;
- baggage-service cutoffs and participating properties;
- hotel policies, meal deadlines, tattoo rules, and accessibility details;
- seasonal events and any claimed best-view period.

## Visual-Evidence Rule

Official photographs and maps may be inspected to verify landform,
architecture, transport livery, sightlines, visitor scale, and current site
arrangement. They are not copied into the book unless an explicit license and
attribution path has passed review. Generated figures must not invent signs,
reconstruct uncertain history, guarantee Fuji visibility, erase safety
barriers, or combine landmarks that cannot share one viewpoint.
