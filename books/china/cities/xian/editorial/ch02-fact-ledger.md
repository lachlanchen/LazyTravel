# Chapter 2 Fact Ledger: Successive Capitals, Different Sites

Ledger date: `2026-08-14`. Destination gate: `china/cities/xian`.

This ledger controls the claims used in Chapter 2. *Xi'an Shihua* supplies the
long historical sequence and research leads. Official heritage records,
gazetteers, the conservation plan, and UNESCO material are used to narrow and
check those leads. The open guides are used only for dated traveler orientation;
their prose and route structure are not reused.

## Rejected Or Deferred Claims

| Claim | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| "Xi'an is one ancient city that changed names through thirteen dynasties." | Rejected. Dynasty counts vary and erase the different locations and forms of successive capitals. |
| "The translucent map fields are archaeological boundaries." | Rejected. They are declared research generalizations for relative orientation only. |
| "Xianyang Palace, Epang Palace, and the Terracotta Army are one attraction." | Rejected. They belong to distinct palace and funerary sites across a broad Qin landscape. |
| "Today's old town preserves the Tang grid." | Rejected without qualification. Some positional continuities survive, but contraction and later rebuilding changed the city. |
| "A reconstructed-looking structure proves the historical appearance of a palace." | Rejected. Archaeological evidence, conservation presentation, later building, and generated editorial imagery remain visibly separate. |
| Current opening hours, tickets, closures, metro exits, and hotel advice. | Deferred to dated attraction, transport, hotel, and departure chapters. |
