## Travel Question

The chapter answers one question: **how should the last Lanzhou day be built
backward from the exact booked station or terminal so that the next Gansu leg
starts without sacrificing the ticket to one more city stop?**

The next destination matters, but it does not identify the departure point by
itself. The live rail or flight booking controls. A westbound traveler may find
useful services from Lanzhou West, another booked train may name Lanzhou
Station, and a flight begins with a separate journey to Zhongchuan T3. The
chapter therefore prints decisions and verification routes, never a preserved
timetable.

## Rejected Shortcuts

- “All westbound trains leave from Lanzhou West.” The ticket, not the compass,
  identifies the departure station.
- “High-speed trains use Lanzhou West and ordinary trains use Lanzhou
  Station.” Service patterns are not a reliable station test.
- A table of current trains, flight numbers, fares, or last departures. It
  becomes stale faster than the book can be reviewed.
- Counting the scheduled train or flight time as the moment to reach the
  building. Stations and terminals include walking, checks, queues, and a
  closing process before departure.
- Visiting the museum from the centre, returning to a western hotel for bags,
  and then crossing the city again for Lanzhou Station.
- Treating T3 as another central-city stop because it has an integrated
  transport centre. Reaching the airport remains a separate journey.
- Adding Zhangye, Jiayuguan, and Dunhuang to one “next stop” line without
  deciding where the next night will actually be spent.
- Turning a temporary holiday or summer train into a year-round recommendation.
- Promising assistance, a lift route, or an accessible transfer from one
  service label. The complete route must be confirmed for each leg.
- Keeping one last attraction after the transfer margin has already begun to
  fail. The city stop is optional; the booked departure is not.

5. **Figure:** use Lanzhou Station when printed on the ticket; connect an
   eastern lodging segment to that gate without a final cross-city detour.
6. **Figure:** treat a T3 flight as a separate airport journey; verify the
   terminal, airline procedure, and the complete transfer.
7. **Practical:** fix the bag-collection point, hard leaving time, and booked
   departure point before keeping one final stop in the same city segment.
8. **Figure:** use an airport-area night only when it protects sleep and an
   early flight; a transfer promise must be confirmed directly.
9. **Practical:** handle peak demand, disruption, mobility assistance, and a
   failed buffer through official channels, one leg at a time.
10. **Callout:** complete one departure card: exact station or terminal,
    document, bags, internal margin, hotel-leaving time, and first fallback.

the airport night; more would repeat the arrival, lodging, and itinerary
