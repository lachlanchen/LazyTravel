# Lanzhou Nearby-day Choice Plate

Create a high-resolution, horizontal, realistic editorial travel image for a
B6 pocket guide. Show a quiet Lanzhou hotel breakfast table just after dawn,
with the close dry valley hills visible through a bright window. The practical
subject is choosing one nearby day by its complete return route. On the table
are three plain, differently colored route cards with no writing, a phone with
a blank map-like screen, four compact day bags, water, a simple breakfast, and
the patchwork travel notebook. The group is ready to leave, but is still making
one decision rather than trying to combine all three routes.

Include exactly the four recurring travel-guide characters shown in the four
supplied references, each once and clearly distinct. Aya-chan is the red-panda
mascot in a navy sailor outfit with a small brown satchel. Lala Xia is the round
panda mascot in cream overalls. Sasa-kun is the human boy in a black-and-white
panda hoodie. The Zhuangzi robot is the tall white humanoid friend with black
joints and a camera-like head. Match the references closely; Aya-chan and Lala
Xia are mascots, Sasa-kun is human, and the robot is a full friend. Arrange all
four around the same table, looking at the three blank cards and one return
route on the phone. Keep them secondary to the clear planning action.

The four guides are the only people or character-like figures anywhere. No
hotel worker, diner, reflected person, distant pedestrian, extra mascot,
duplicated guide, map label, ticket text, clock digits, brand, caption, border,
watermark, or pseudo-lettering. Do not show the three destinations in one
collage and do not imply that all three can be visited in a day. Use a clean
16:9 composition with vivid but natural vermilion, jade, cobalt, and coral
accents. Avoid yellow wash, sepia, dark mood, fantasy travel portals, oversized
characters, and clutter. Leave safe edge space for the print crop.
