## Travel Question

The chapter answers one question: **which single nearby branch has a confirmed
outward trip, entrance, internal movement, return, and cancellation rule on the
actual travel date?**

It does not rank scenery or build a regional loop. Bingling Temple and
Liujiaxia, Xinglong Mountain, and Lanzhou Ink Danxia are alternatives. The
Yellow River-Tao River confluence viewpoint is a conditional road branch
inside the Liujiaxia decision, never a fourth day trip or an assumed part of a
Bingling boat journey.

## Rejected Shortcuts

- Joining Bingling, the confluence, Xinglong, and Ink Danxia into one circuit.
- Treating arrival at Liujiaxia as arrival at Bingling Temple Grottoes.
- Copying a `2014` fare, sailing, bus, road time, or admission rule into a
  `2026` itinerary.
- Combining a one-way boat with an unconfirmed road pickup at the grottoes.
- Assuming the confluence viewpoint is included in a Bingling boat ticket.
- Promising a fixed blue-and-yellow river boundary or saturated Danxia color.
- Treating the giant Buddha and Cave 169 as the same location on the cliff.
- Reading an old map-app opening label as proof that Xinglong has recovered.
- Inferring reopening from construction, a recent photograph, or the absence
  of a closure notice.
- Using one accessibility label as a complete hotel-to-viewpoint guarantee.
- Replacing a failed morning branch with a second unverified nearby branch.
- Making one restaurant or local dish the hinge of the return journey.
