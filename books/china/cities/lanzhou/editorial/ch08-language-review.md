# Chapter 8 Language Review

Status: Chinese, Japanese, and English accepted on `2026-08-22`; compiled B6
and `390 px` responsive reading review pass.

## Shared Route

All ten blocks answer one booking question: which lodging segment makes both
arrival night and the first fixed stop next morning short and explainable? The
chapter moves west, centre, east, then to the airport exception. Four current
properties demonstrate how to verify an address and direct contact; they are
not a ranking or a promise about the room sold.

History and sightseeing remain attached to the route already built in earlier
chapters. The bridge and old centre explain the central segment, the museum and
Lanzhou West explain the western segment, and Lanzhou Station and the university
side explain the eastern segment. No disconnected hotel catalogue interrupts
that line.

## Chinese

The Chinese pass now uses ordinary booking language: `抵达点到酒店`, `次日第一项
固定安排`, `晚饭后步行回酒店`, and `付款前的最后核对`. It removed stiff forms such
as `第一处不能改的目的地`, `把晚饭接回住宿`, and `各走一次纸面路线`. The final pass
also changed the map rule from the unnatural `到达口` to `抵达点`, asks where
luggage can actually be left, and treats old foreign-guest lists as obsolete
evidence rather than present conditions.

## Japanese

The Japanese pass was edited independently around normal travel decisions:
`翌朝最初の固定予定`, `夕食後に歩いて宿へ戻りやすい`, `チェックアウト後`, and
`支払い前の最終確認`. It removed literal forms such as `動かせない目的地`,
replaced `窓下` with `窓のすぐ下`, and uses `ナイトマーケット` where that is
more natural than a forced kanji reading. Registration and accessibility use
normal Japanese terms rather than Chinese calques.

## English

The English pass leads with a fixed arrival point and first morning stop. It
replaced `first immovable stop`, `join dinner to the walk home`, `room sold`,
and `recovery margin` with direct traveler language. Each property paragraph
distinguishes what the operator page establishes from what must still be asked:
entrance, room side, transfer procedure, luggage, and the exact booking terms.

## Alignment Result

The three languages align by segment, reason, dated property check, live
verification, and booking stop rule rather than sentence shape. None promises
a quiet room, river view, exact travel time, airport pickup, foreign-guest desk
procedure, or complete accessible route. B6 and responsive review found no
language, ruby, clipping, caption, or final-size legibility failure. The prose
is closed unless a later factual or rendering defect is demonstrated.
