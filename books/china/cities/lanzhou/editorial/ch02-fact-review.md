## Chapter Job

The chapter prevents one expensive arrival mistake: treating Zhongchuan
Airport, Lanzhou West Railway Station, and Lanzhou Railway Station as
interchangeable entrances. The reader should finish with one booked gate, one
first city segment, one transfer method that still operates at the actual
arrival time, and a fallback that does not depend on a tight connection.

This is not a transport encyclopaedia. Train numbers, fares, first and last
services, airport-bus routes, platform assignments, exits, pickup zones, and
journey times stay outside durable prose. They are checked through the named
operator immediately before travel.

## Factual Boundaries

- `中川机场东` is the railway station physically connected to T3. It is not a
  synonym for the airport terminal, and a rail ticket is not implied by a
  flight booking.
- The official 2026 airport page confirms rail and road branches but does not
  make one branch best for every arrival. Late flights, baggage reclaim, ticket
  availability, mobility, and the final address change the decision.
- `兰州西站北广场` is the metro-station name used by the current operator. It
  is related to, but not textually identical with, the national-rail station
  `兰州西站`.
- The Line 2 metro stop is `兰州火车站`; the national-rail ticket commonly uses
  `兰州站`. The book shows both labels where the distinction helps a traveler.
- The chapter does not classify all high-speed trains at one station and all
  conventional trains at the other. The train-specific 12306 result decides.
- Accessibility, elevators, station assistance, taxi queues, road traffic,
  construction, and pickup zones are checked for the actual trip.
