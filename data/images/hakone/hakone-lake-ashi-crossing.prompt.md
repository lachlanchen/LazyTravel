# Hakone Lake Ashi Crossing Figure Brief

Use case: photorealistic-natural

Create a high-resolution realistic editorial travel image in a `3:2` landscape
composition from the open passenger deck of a contemporary Lake Ashi
sightseeing vessel travelling south from Togendai. The main subjects are the
lake surface, layered wooded caldera ridges, a receding north shore, low cloud,
and the boat's safe deck railing with a restrained hint of its decorative mast.
Show wind patterns on the water and enough wake to communicate motion. Do not
show Mount Fuji; the crossing must remain valuable without it.

Show exactly the four recurring LazyTravel guides, using all four supplied
identity references:

- Aya-chan, the red-panda girl in her established navy sailor outfit;
- Lala Xia, the taller panda companion in her established light travel outfit;
- Sasa-kun, the smaller child companion in the established panda hood;
- Zhuangzi, the white articulated humanoid robot with a plain chest panel.

Keep all four behind the deck railing and small in the lower third. Aya-chan
reads the shore, Lala Xia watches the cloud base, Sasa-kun looks back towards
the shrinking port, and Zhuangzi holds the patchwork notebook closed against
the breeze. Their clothing and hair should react mildly and consistently to
the wind. Preserve each face, outfit, body, and relative height. Use crisp
natural daylight, vivid but credible cobalt and jade, clean whites and reds,
realistic water texture, and documentary depth suitable for a full B6 page at
300 dpi.

Use the supplied official cruise frames only to keep the deck, vessel, water,
and ridgeline relationship credible. Create a new scene rather than copying a
camera frame.

Prohibited: any fifth person; duplicated or hybrid guides; missing Aya-chan or
Lala Xia; anyone outside the railing or climbing rigging; a sailing ship at
sea; tropical water; generic European mountains; giant waves; guaranteed Mount
Fuji; readable signs, logos, flags with text, pseudo-Japanese text, or
watermarks; dull sepia or yellow grading; mascot pose, anime rendering, or
painterly style.
