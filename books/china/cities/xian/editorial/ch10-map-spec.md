# Chapter 10 Map Spec: Nested Itinerary Days

## Reader Question

How can a two-day first visit become a three- or five-day visit without
repeating transfers or turning each day into a checklist?

## Required Structure

- the two-day route contains Day 1 inside the wall and Day 2 at the Qin
  mausoleum museum;
- the three-day route adds one southern museum-and-pagoda day;
- the five-day route adds one nearby place on Day 4 and a flexible city-depth
  or recovery day on Day 5;
- Day 3 shows the Small Wild Goose Pagoda and Xi'an Museum as a replacement
  when the primary museum booking fails, not as extra stops;
- Day 4 shows Huashan, Han Yangling, Cuihuashan, and Qianling as mutually
  exclusive choices;
- Day 5 uses Daming Palace only as an example of a coherent city-depth thread.

## Layout

- wide landscape master for the B6 map page;
- one urban panel for Days 1, 3, and 5;
- one regional panel for Day 2 and the four Day 4 choices;
- a large nested-plan band and five large trilingual day cards;
- vivid coral, berry, cobalt, jade, and saffron day colors on white;
- labels remain readable in the B6 print proof and the website's 390 px map
  viewport after zoom.

## Explicit Limits

- sequence lines are not walking, road, metro, rail, cableway, or shuttle
  geometry;
- regional dotted lines show relative direction only;
- no journey times, opening hours, ticket prices, reservation windows, or
  complete transport network;
- no day card promises that every listed optional stop will fit;
- real entry points, closures, transport, weather, and bookings require a
  current check.

## QA Gates

- exactly five day cards in order and exactly four mutually exclusive Day 4
  choices;
- the nested two-, three-, and five-day rule is visible without reading the
  prose page;
- all labels and cards remain distinct with no clipping or collision;
- SVG text remains selectable, PDF output remains vector, and the PNG master
  is at least 6,000 px wide;
- B6 and mobile proofs are visually inspected before approval.
