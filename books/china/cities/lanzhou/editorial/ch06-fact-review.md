## Travel Question

The chapter answers one question: how can a traveler eat a distinctly Lanzhou
day without turning local food into a checklist or arriving after the meal has
lost its natural setting?

The route is fixed:

`morning beef-noodle shop -> one clear counter order -> midday reset -> one
afternoon snack choice -> lily and covered-bowl tea when the setting suits ->
stop before dinner becomes another list`

This is a clock rather than a restaurant ranking. It explains what to decide,
what to look for in the bowl or dish, and what to ask at the counter. It does
not freeze prices, opening hours, branch quality, recipes, dietary status, or
availability into the book.

## Locked Block Sequence

1. **Figure:** a morning noodle-shop table with one readable Lanzhou bowl and
   all four guides; the bowl and ordinary shop rhythm remain dominant.
2. **Practical:** go early, check the actual shop, and ask direct dietary or
   halal questions rather than relying on assumptions.
3. **Diagram:** follow the common pay-or-ticket, choose-width, add-extra,
   collect, season-and-sit sequence while yielding to the shop's own signs.
4. **Bowl reading:** connect noodle width, pulled-dough craft, clear broth,
   radish, chilli oil, greens, and optional additions without repeating an
   origin legend or ranking authenticity.
5. **Diagram:** read the day in broad phases: morning bowl, midday reset,
   afternoon snack or tea, evening stop or flexible fallback.
6. **Figure:** place `酿皮子`, `灰豆子`, and `甜醅子` together as three visibly
   different choices, with the four guides secondary to the food.
7. **Choice method:** distinguish dressed strips, a dark sweet pea bowl, and
   fermented grain; ask about condiments and fermentation when relevant.
8. **Figure:** show edible white lily-bulb scales beside one covered-bowl
   `三炮台`, with all four guides and no anonymous hands.
9. **Ingredient check:** separate GI origin from a menu label, and ask about
   tea ingredients, sugar, and caffeine instead of promising one recipe.
10. **Stop rule:** complete a realistic day with one bowl, one snack decision,
    and at most one tea or lily stop; do not cross the city to complete a list.

Exactly ten aligned blocks are required. Five visual blocks keep the chapter
usable as a pocket guide. Nine would merge food recognition with a dietary
