# Chapter 9 Fact Ledger: Choose the District First

Ledger date: `2026-08-16`. Destination gate: `china/cities/xian`.

This chapter treats a hotel as part of the itinerary rather than a rating,
price band, or generic city-centre search. The supplied 2013 family guide and
the pinned Chinese and English open guides are useful evidence that Xi'an has
long been sold through central, pagoda-side, station, and Lintong lodging
clusters. Their rates, route numbers, phone numbers, business descriptions,
and several named properties are obsolete and are not reused as current
advice. Official hotel pages, current transport sources, and national
accommodation rules control the live examples checked for this edition.

## Rejected Or Deferred Claims

| Claim | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Bell Tower is the best district for every first-time visitor. | Rejected. It is strong for an old-core itinerary, not automatically for Yanta-heavy days, an early northern train, or a Lintong sequence. |
| A higher star rating compensates for a poor district choice. | Rejected. Star class does not remove repeated cross-city travel or a difficult final entrance. |
| “Near Xi'an North” means walkable to the railway concourse. | Rejected. Search radii, straight-line distance, road barriers, station plazas, and pickup zones differ. Test the exact final leg. |
| Lintong is a convenient base for seeing Xi'an as a whole. | Rejected. It is an eastern itinerary choice, not a substitute for the city base. |
| Old guide prices, phone numbers, cash-only warnings, hostel lists, or route numbers remain current. | Rejected. They are retained only as evidence of how quickly practical listings age. |
| Every room in a named hotel has the view, window, bed, bathroom, or smoking status shown in a representative photograph. | Rejected. Confirm the booked room category in writing. |
| Hotels may lawfully refuse foreign guests because they lack a special “foreign qualification.” | Rejected as a customer-facing rule. Current national policy directs platforms and operators not to use such qualification barriers; the hotel handles registration. |
| A split stay always saves travel time. | Rejected. The move itself consumes time and attention; use it only for a clear rail or two-day eastern reason. |
| Live prices, ratings, availability, breakfast hours, and cancellation deadlines belong in the durable chapter. | Rejected. They belong in the dated booking check. |

## Language Pass Briefs

- **Chinese:** use `城墙内`, `永宁门（南门）`, `雁塔／曲江`, `西安北站片区`,
  and `临潼` consistently. Prefer `临街房`, `背街房`, `实际入口`, and
  `订单所含房型`. Avoid `闭眼订`, `天花板`, `性价比之王`, `核心C位`, and
  hotel-platform sales language.
- **Japanese:** use ordinary lodging terms: `城壁内`, `南門`, `雁塔・曲江`,
  `西安北駅周辺`, `臨潼`, `通り側の部屋`, `実際の入口`, and `予約した客室
  タイプ`. Do not translate Chinese platform slang or property marketing copy.
- **English:** prefer “inside the wall,” “South Gate,” “Yanta/Qujiang,”
  “Xi'an North corridor,” “Lintong,” “street-facing room,” “actual entrance,”
  and “booked room type.” Avoid “perfect base,” “hidden gem,” “seamless,”
  “luxury oasis,” “heart of everything,” and affiliate-review language.

## Visual Evidence Rules

- `asset-xian-stay-areas-map` is character-free. It shows five planning zones,
  a central inset, and concise trilingual fit checks. It is not a complete
  metro, street route, room inventory, price map, or endorsement layer.
- `asset-xian-south-gate-hotel-arrival` includes Aya-chan, Lala Xia, Sasa-kun,
  and the Zhuangzi robot as equal guide travelers. Aya and Lala must be fully
  recognizable. Yongning Gate and the wall remain the dominant Xi'an signal;
  the hotel entrance is contemporary, plausible, and unbranded.
- The scene shows a useful action: the group pauses with compact luggage while
  one guide checks the exact entrance and another checks the booking. No one
  blocks the doorway, pavement, or vehicle path.
- Generated signs must not contain readable hotel names, prices, ratings,
  platform marks, QR codes, room numbers, or invented Chinese directions.
- Reader-facing captions explain the lodging decision. Generation method,
  prompt, references, checksums, and QA remain in technical provenance.
