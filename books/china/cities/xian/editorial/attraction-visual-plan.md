# Xi'an Attraction Visual Plan

Status: active editorial gate, 2026-08-16.

The visual rhythm serves the trip. Maps explain choice and movement;
attraction-dominant figures let the reader recognize a place and understand
its scale; instructional figures explain one action. An instructional scene
does not replace a view of the destination.

For every non-map figure, Aya-chan and Lala Xia remain visible and
recognizable. New location plates use the full four-guide team when the
composition remains legible: Aya-chan, Lala Xia, Sasa-kun, and the Zhuangzi
robot occupy the foreground or lower edge as traveler-scale guides while the
place remains the clear subject. Raw source and web photographs stay in
ignored research storage and are used only as factual shape references; no
reference image is republished.

## Acceptance Rules

1. The attraction occupies more visual attention than the guides or props.
2. Architectural levels, silhouettes, site boundaries, and visitor viewpoint
   are checked against official or institutional visual references.
3. The image does not reconstruct a vanished building, mix dynasties, invent
   signage, or present a representative scene as one exact viewpoint.
4. Captions say what the reader should notice at the place. Production method,
   reference hashes, rights, and factual limits stay in provenance.
5. Original resolution, B6 proof, and 390 px website proof must all pass before
   the asset enters canonical JSON.
6. A visual cadence is not a quota. Add a location plate when it helps the
   reader recognize, compare, or move through a place; stop before repeated
   views turn the pocket guide into a crowded picture book.
