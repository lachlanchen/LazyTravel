# Chapter 5 Language Review

Status: Chinese, Japanese, and English accepted on `2026-08-22` after
independent line edits and compiled B6/mobile review.

## Shared Route

All ten blocks serve one museum visit: recognize the building, verify current
entry and display conditions, go to the third floor, read Painted Pottery and
Buddhist Art, descend to the second-floor Silk Road gallery, then leave with one
question for the next Gansu leg. Objects stay attached to Qin'an, Gangu,
Jingchuan, Wuwei, Jiayuguan, or Dunhuang rather than becoming Lanzhou history by
virtue of display.

## Chinese

The Chinese pass uses direct museum and travel language: `先认`, `先看当天开放`,
`只在体力、兴趣和关门前时间允许时加一个`, and `看完核心路线就离馆`. The practical
box says `放票时间以预约页为准` because the official record conflicts with
itself; it does not hide the conflict behind a guessed time. Phrases that would
turn motifs into myths, call every object a treasure, or reduce the Silk Road to
a slogan were removed.

## Japanese

The Japanese pass was edited as an independent guide. It uses natural museum
terms such as `資料`, `開室状況`, `出土地`, and `展示替え`, and frames missing
objects with `なければ別の器で同じ手順を使えばよい`. Chinese place and object
names remain identifiable, while the sentence order follows Japanese movement
and observation rather than Chinese syntax. `東西交流` is explicitly rejected
as a substitute for looking.

## English

The English pass keeps the traveler and the evidence in view: check the live
appointment screen, make one ascent and one descent, read one vessel in four
passes, and separate what the label states from what the traveler infers. It
uses the museum's `bronze galloping horse` title without fixing the bird as a
swallow. Generic museum language such as “East meets West,” “must-see treasure,”
and “journey through time” does not appear.

## Alignment Result

The three languages align by route, object, and decision rather than sentence
shape. Each distinguishes collection location from findspot, makes named-object
availability conditional, separates durable object history from current
booking rules, and treats one carefully read object as a complete result. The
captions describe what to recognize or do; production details remain in
provenance.
