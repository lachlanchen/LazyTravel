# Chapter 7 Fact Ledger: Around Xi'an, Give One Place a Day

Ledger date: `2026-08-15`. Destination gate: `china/cities/xian`.

This chapter is a decision chapter, not a second attraction catalogue. The
2013 family guide and the pinned Chinese and English open guides are useful for
identifying the familiar east, west, mountain, and Qinling choices. Their old
bus numbers, prices, rankings, superlatives, legends, and attempts to join too
many sites in one day are not publishable travel advice. Current museum,
scenic-area, railway, municipal, and conservation sources control access and
transport claims. *Xi'an Shihua* supplies historical leads for the imperial
landscape; it is not used for present-day route planning or for claims that
were conjectural when the book was written.

| `ch07-b006` | Huashan route logic | Railway, local transfer, visitor centre, scenic bus, cableway, and walking form one chain. Choose a modest cableway route before buying its separate components. |
| `ch07-b007` | Mountain-day callout | Weather, wind, visibility, crowd controls, cableway operation, footwear, water, and a return margin decide the day. Turning back is a route decision, not a failure. |
| `ch07-b008` | Han Yangling | A quieter Han archaeology day centred on the underground external-pit display and open site scale. Road access must be planned; it is not an automatic airport stop. |
| `ch07-b009` | Qinling foothill | Cuihuashan is chosen for managed access and legible landslide landforms. Stay inside the scenic boundary; do not publish wild-crossing routes. |
| `ch07-b010` | Western imperial landscape | Qianling main approach, stone figures, and one associated museum or tomb form one field day. Do not turn western Shaanxi into a cemetery checklist. |
| `ch07-b011` | Figure | Aya-chan, Lala Xia, Sasa-kun, and the Zhuangzi robot on a managed mountain route; show that cableway access still leaves steps, exposure, and weather. |
| `ch07-b012` | Final practical protocol | Night-before and morning-of checks, a return buffer, and weather substitutions. Commit to one destination and cut the optional stop first. |

The block count is fixed at `12`. The chapter must remain a finite choice
system. Extra place names, duplicate summaries, and mini-guides to every tomb
or Qinling valley are prohibited.

## Rejected Or Deferred Claims

| Claim | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Huashan, Lintong, Han Yangling, Cuihuashan, and Qianling are all easy “nearby” stops that can be combined because they fit on one regional map. | Rejected. They lie in different directions and have different transfer, terrain, booking, and return constraints. |
| A first Huashan day should conquer all five peaks, walk the plank road, or stay for sunrise. | Rejected as default advice. These are separate risk, fitness, weather, and accommodation decisions. |
| Taking a cableway makes Huashan a low-effort sightseeing stop. | Rejected. Official routes still include scenic buses, queues, stairs, walking, and exposed mountain terrain. |
| The Qin mausoleum and Huaqing Palace tell one continuous “imperial love and power” story. | Rejected. Their dates, evidence, surviving fabric, and political contexts are distinct. |
| Present Huaqing buildings are an intact Tang palace, or the commercial evening performance is historical evidence. | Rejected. Archaeological remains, later architecture, modern landscape design, and staged performance must stay separate. |
| Han Yangling is simply a smaller or less impressive Terracotta Army. | Rejected. Its scale, burial organization, figures, animals, and display method answer different questions about Western Han imperial life. |
| Cuihuashan is a gateway to publishable “secret” Qinling crossings. | Rejected. The chapter uses a managed scenic area and repeats the legal boundary, not social-media trail lore. |
| Qianling's unexcavated chamber contains a known weight of treasure, the missing heads of the envoy figures have one settled explanation, or every legend in the family/open guides is historical fact. | Rejected. These claims exceed the evidence or remain disputed. |
| Qianling, Zhaoling, Maoling, Famen Temple, and every associated tomb form one reasonable western day. | Rejected. Chapter 7 gives Qianling one coherent day and leaves the other landscapes for later trips. |
| Old bus numbers, seasonal opening times, ticket prices, cableway prices, station schedules, or travel times from the supplied and open guides. | Rejected as current advice. These sources remain useful only as dated leads and contradiction checks. |
| Named hotels at Huashan, Lintong, or Qianling. | Deferred to Chapter 9 and only after current verification. Chapter 7 assumes a return to Xi'an unless the traveler deliberately plans an overnight stay. |

## Language Pass Briefs

- **Chinese:** use `临潼`, `秦始皇帝陵博物院`, `丽山园`, `华清宫`,
  `华山`, `汉景帝阳陵博物院`, `翠华山`, and `乾陵` precisely. Avoid
  `一天拿下`, `勇者必打卡`, `秘境穿越`, `帝王传奇`, and promotional
  scenic-area slogans.
- **Japanese:** distinguish `華山` from generic `山`, and `漢陽陵` from the
  Qin museum without comparison language. Explain `外蔵坑`, `神道`, and
  `山崩地形` in ordinary Japanese. Avoid translated Chinese tourism slogans.
- **English:** use "external pits," "managed scenic boundary," "southern
  approach," and "associated tomb" where needed. Avoid "conquer," "hidden
  gem," "bucket list," "easy add-on," "mysterious treasures," and "off the
  beaten path."

## Visual Evidence Rules

- `asset-xian-nearby-day-choices-map` is character-free. It places the five
  choices around central Xi'an, uses broad burden classes, and explicitly says
  that its connectors are choice directions rather than live routes.
- The map uses pinned OpenStreetMap positions and official destination
  addresses. It contains no fares, opening times, hotels, wild trails, or
  promise that a particular connection is operating.
- `asset-xian-managed-mountain-day` contains Aya-chan and Lala Xia at minimum;
  the preferred clear composition includes Sasa-kun and the Zhuangzi robot as
  equal guides. It shows a managed mountain path with railings, stone steps,
  changing cloud, and practical day packs. It must not reproduce a dangerous
  attraction, a precise Huashan viewpoint, or a fake sign.
- Reader-facing captions explain mountain-day judgment. Prompt, generator,
  reference paths, checksums, rights, and production QA remain in technical
  provenance and do not interrupt the reading experience.
