## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.

Create a high-resolution horizontal 3:2 editorial travel image of a quiet
present-day visit to the Big Wild Goose Pagoda at Da Ci'en Temple in Xi'an.
The actual attraction must dominate: one square, gently tapering, seven-storey
ochre-brown brick pagoda, with a small finial, restrained brick eaves, one
arched opening centred on each visible face and no fantasy roof tiers. Frame it
from a temple courtyard at a believable human eye level. Include a modest band
of paving, a few mature trees, and clear blue daylight so readers can inspect
the tower's mass and vertical rhythm. This is the existing tower, not a Tang
city reconstruction.

Place exactly two recurring guides, Aya-chan and Lala Xia, together near the
lower-right or lower-left edge. Preserve both supplied identities precisely.
Show coherent full bodies, ground contact, natural shadows, and gentle
three-quarter faces so each remains recognizable at B6 size. They look up at
the same tower and occupy no more than roughly one fifth of the image height.
The pagoda, not the characters, is the subject.

Style: realistic documentary travel photography with tactile character guides,
natural brick, stone, foliage, fabric, and fur detail; vivid but balanced
vermilion, jade, cobalt, and coral accents; no yellow cast or cinematic haze.

Constraints: exactly seven visible pagoda storeys; square brick form; exactly
two guides and no anonymous visitors; no Xuanzang statue as the foreground
subject; no invented plaque, readable text, logo, caption, watermark, souvenir
pose, dramatic sunset, festival lights, modern fantasy mall, reconstructed
Tang palace, extra pagoda, duplicate character, malformed paws, clipped ears,
or cut-off feet.
