## Travel Question

The chapter answers one question: which part of Lanzhou should hold the
traveler's bed so that arrival and the next morning form one usable route?

The decision uses two fixed movements before it considers a hotel brand:

`arrival gate -> lodging entrance`

`lodging entrance -> first fixed stop next morning`

Four alternatives are sufficient:

`Lanzhou West or museum morning -> Qilihe and the western segment`

`bridge, old centre, and dinner on foot -> Xiguan and the central segment`

`Lanzhou Station or university-side morning -> Dongfanghong and the eastern segment`

`late landing or early flight -> one airport-area buffer night`

The airport area is not treated as a fifth city centre. A named property is a
dated example that gives the reader a direct page, address, and contact route;
it is not a ranking, endorsement, or promise about the room eventually sold.

## Rejected Claims

- `One Lanzhou district is best for every traveler.` Rejected: the arrival gate
  and first fixed stop decide the useful segment.
- `A central hotel is beside the river, quiet, or convenient for every route.`
  Rejected: “central” covers different streets and exposures.
- `A hotel brand, star category, review score, or search ranking proves quality.`
  Rejected: none proves the exact room, entrance, cleanliness, noise, or service
  on the travel date.
- `A room photograph proves the view or layout of the room sold.` Rejected:
  operator pages themselves may label photographs non-contractual; generated
  scenes are illustrative only.
- `Wheelchair accessible` or an accessibility filter proves a step-free stay.
  Rejected: confirm the complete route and the exact room.
- `Airport shuttle` proves a vehicle will meet every flight at every terminal.
  Rejected: confirm pickup, reservation, frequency, capacity, and first/last
  usable trip.
- `Old guides show which Lanzhou hotels accept foreigners.` Rejected as current
  advice. Use current law, a direct property channel, and written confirmation.
- Exact rates, breakfast hours, deposits, cancellation windows, check-in times,
  metro exits, drive times, walking times, and shuttle schedules are omitted
  unless they are needed for a dated trip and reconfirmed before payment.
