# Hakone Togendai Harbor Choice Figure Brief

Use case: photorealistic-natural

Create a high-resolution realistic editorial travel image in a `3:2` landscape
composition. The main subject is the contemporary Togendai harbor threshold on
Lake Ashi: a sheltered boarding area, clean pier railings, the lake and wooded
ridges beyond, and one current gold-and-red sightseeing vessel waiting at a
safe berth. The place and the practical choice must dominate the frame. It is
not a portrait and not a fantasy harbor.

Show exactly the four recurring LazyTravel guides, using all four supplied
identity references:

- Aya-chan, the red-panda girl in her established navy sailor outfit;
- Lala Xia, the taller panda companion in her established light travel outfit;
- Sasa-kun, the smaller child companion in the established panda hood;
- Zhuangzi, the white articulated humanoid robot with a plain chest panel.

Place all four together but naturally near the side of the sheltered queue,
small enough that the harbor remains the subject. Aya-chan and Lala Xia compare
the boat at the berth with an unlabelled route card. Sasa-kun looks towards the
lake. Zhuangzi points towards the correct boarding lane without touching any
barrier. Preserve each face, outfit, body, and relative height. Use bright
overcast or clear-soft daylight, vivid credible lake blue, forest green, clean
vermillion and gold accents, neutral concrete, and documentary depth suitable
for a full B6 page at 300 dpi.

Use the supplied official cruise frames only to keep the vessel, pier, ridge,
and working harbor relationship credible. Create a new scene; do not reproduce
one exact frame, signage, staff uniforms, or branding.

Prohibited: any fifth person; duplicated or hybrid guides; missing Aya-chan or
Lala Xia; open water access outside barriers; standing on a gangway; invented
European port architecture; dramatic storm; guaranteed Mount Fuji; readable
signs, timetables, logos, numbers, pseudo-Japanese text, or watermarks; dull
sepia or yellow grading; mascot pose, anime rendering, or painterly style.
