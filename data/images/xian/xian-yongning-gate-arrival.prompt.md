## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.

Asset type: horizontal opening editorial figure for a B6 multilingual Xi'an
travel guide and responsive website.

Create a new realistic daytime travel-guide view of Yongning Gate, the South
Gate complex of Xi'an City Wall, as the threshold between the walled historic
core and the modern city. Show the broad grey-brick Ming ramparts, crenellated
wall, axial gate openings, layered gate towers with grey tiled upturned roofs
and muted red timber, the surrounding green edge, and restrained modern city
buildings beyond. The place and its urban scale must dominate.

Preserve the four supplied guide identities exactly and include exactly one of
each: Aya-chan, the red-panda schoolgirl in navy sailor uniform; Lala Xia, the
round panda in cream overalls; Sasa-kun, the boy in a panda hoodie; and the
white-and-black articulated Zhuangzi robot. Treat the robot as a full member of
the group. Place all four small on a safe pedestrian plaza in the lower
foreground, walking toward the gate from behind or in three-quarter view. They
do not pose, block traffic, climb masonry, or enter restricted space.

Composition: 3:2 landscape from a slightly elevated but plausible
pedestrian-plaza viewpoint outside the southern wall. Use the Xinhua aerial
only to understand the layered gate-and-wall relationship, then create an
original ground-level composition. The rampart and gate complex take at least
80 percent of visual attention. Keep the complete silhouette readable at B6
size and allow modern buildings to remain visible as context. Use clear
late-morning summer light, vivid green trees, neutral grey brick, a natural
blue sky, and deep focus.

Constraints: exactly four guide characters, no duplicates. Preserve each
character's clothing, shape, and materials. Show the present-day site, not a
dynasty reconstruction. No festival decorations, ceremony, unsafe roadway
position, text, label, logo, watermark, banner, fantasy palace addition,
bright red wall masonry, historic costume, orange cast, excessive crowd,
blurred gate, shallow depth of field, or oversized mascot.
