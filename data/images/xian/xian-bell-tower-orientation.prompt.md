## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.

Asset type: horizontal full-width editorial figure for a B6 multilingual
Xi'an travel guide and responsive website.

Create a new realistic street-level travel-guide view of Xi'an Bell Tower at
the junction of the four main avenues. The historic building must dominate: a
substantial square masonry base with arched passages, the centered Ming timber
tower above it, green glazed tiles, richly painted bracket sets, and the
distinctive triple-eaved pyramidal roof. Show the broad modern intersection and
four-direction urban axis clearly enough that the reader understands the tower
as a city-centre orientation point.

Preserve the four supplied guide identities exactly and include exactly one of
each: Aya-chan, the red-panda schoolgirl in navy sailor uniform; Lala Xia, the
round panda in cream overalls; Sasa-kun, the boy in a panda hoodie; and the
white-and-black articulated Zhuangzi robot. Treat the robot as a full member of
the group. Place all four together, small in the lower foreground at a safe
pedestrian viewing edge, never standing in traffic. They look toward the tower
and city axis rather than posing for the camera.

Composition: 3:2 landscape, natural eye-level perspective from a legal
pedestrian viewpoint. Bell Tower and its masonry base take most of the frame;
the four guides occupy no more than 15 percent. Keep the roof silhouette and
road-axis relationship legible at B6 size. Use a clear blue-hour early evening
with balanced warm tower lights and a cool sky; architectural detail remains
visible and traffic does not hide the building.

Constraints: use the official municipal photograph only as an architectural
shape reference; create an original composition. Exactly four guide
characters, no duplicates. Preserve each character's clothing, shape, and
materials. No invented historic ceremony, unsafe behavior, readable text,
label, logo, watermark, billboard lettering, festival clutter, neon fantasy,
extreme light trails, orange cast, distorted roof levels, extra mascots,
selfie pose, blurred landmark, or shallow depth of field.
