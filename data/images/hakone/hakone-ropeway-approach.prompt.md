# Hakone Ropeway Approach Figure Brief

Create a high-resolution realistic editorial travel image in a `3:2` landscape
composition. The main subject is the real approach from Sounzan toward
Owakudani in a contemporary enclosed Hakone ropeway gondola. View the scene
from just inside the cabin so the large window frames the pale volcanic valley,
wooded ridges, cable line, and one or two distant gondolas. The steep valley and
fumarole field must occupy most of the frame. Do not show Mount Fuji; this scene
is about arriving at the volcano, not winning a weather lottery.

Show exactly the four recurring LazyTravel guides, using all four supplied
identity references:

- Aya-chan, the red-panda girl in her established navy sailor outfit;
- Lala Xia, the taller panda companion in her established light travel outfit;
- Sasa-kun, the smaller child companion in the established panda hood;
- Zhuangzi, the white articulated humanoid robot with a plain chest panel.

Place the four naturally along one side of the cabin, small enough that the
landscape remains dominant. Aya-chan and Lala Xia look down into the valley;
Sasa-kun watches an approaching gondola; Zhuangzi steadies a plain folded route
sheet with only unlabelled coloured lines and dots. Preserve each face, outfit,
body, and relative height. Use clean morning light, vivid but credible cobalt
sky, jade woods, mineral grey and ochre ground, realistic glass reflections,
and documentary depth suitable for a full B6 page at 300 dpi.

Use the supplied official ropeway and Owakudani landscape images only to keep
the cabin, cable, ridge, and valley relationship credible. Create a new scene;
do not reproduce text, logos, advertisements, or one exact photograph.

Prohibited: a fifth traveler or guide; duplicated or hybrid characters;
anonymous faces; open-air or fantasy gondolas; a dramatic eruption, lava, fire,
or unsafe open doors; guaranteed Mount Fuji; readable signs, logos, numbers,
pseudo-Japanese text, or watermarks; dull sepia or yellow grading; mascot pose,
anime rendering, or painterly style.
