# Hakone Lake Ashi Wakasagi Lunch Figure Brief

Use case: photorealistic-natural

Create a high-resolution realistic editorial food-and-place image in a `3:2`
landscape composition at a modest contemporary lakeside dining room. The food
must dominate the foreground: one shared plate of small whole Lake Ashi
wakasagi fried crisp in fine crumbs, a smaller dish of wakasagi nanbanzuke with
thin onion and carrot, rice, miso soup, tea, and simple seasonal pickles. Keep
each fish recognizably small and slender. The lake and wooded ridge may appear
through a clear window, but do not invent a named restaurant or luxury ryokan.

Show exactly the four recurring LazyTravel guides, using all four supplied
identity references:

- Aya-chan, the red-panda girl in her established navy sailor outfit;
- Lala Xia, the taller panda companion in her established light travel outfit;
- Sasa-kun, the smaller companion in the established panda hood;
- Zhuangzi, the white articulated humanoid robot with a plain chest panel.

Seat exactly these four around one uncluttered wooden table. They have paused
before serving so the two wakasagi preparations can be seen clearly; Zhuangzi
sets down a small teapot while the other three look at the meal. Keep faces
unobstructed and hands anatomically plausible. Preserve each face, outfit, body,
and relative height. Use natural window light, appetising golden fry, fresh
vegetable colour, clean blue-green lake notes, neutral tableware, and realistic
food texture suitable for a full B6 page at 300 dpi.

Use the supplied fishery photographs only to keep fish size and the fried and
nanbanzuke preparations credible. Create a new meal scene rather than copying
the plates.

Prohibited: any fifth person; duplicated or hybrid guides; missing Aya-chan or
Lala Xia; trout, sardines, oversized fish, sushi, raw fish, black eggs, generic
ramen, or an overloaded banquet; eating with hands; impossible chopsticks;
invented restaurant signs; readable labels, logos, pseudo-Japanese text, or
watermarks; dull sepia or yellow grading; mascot pose, anime rendering, or
painterly style.
