# Hakone Chapter 2 Arrival Figure Brief

Create a high-resolution horizontal travel-editorial scene for Chapter 2 of a
B6 Hakone guide. The visual job is to show the moment when an intercity journey
becomes a mountain journey: at Hakone-Yumoto, the traveler must deal with the
large bag and choose the first direction before following the crowd.

## Reference Order

1. Aya-chan: preserve the red-panda face, navy sailor outfit, red scarf, brown
   shoes, and small brown case.
2. Lala Xia: preserve the panda form and cream overalls.
3. Sasa-kun: preserve the child in the black-and-white panda hooded outfit.
4. Zhuangzi robot: preserve the white articulated humanoid body and camera-like
   head; omit chest lettering rather than inventing it.
5. Official Hakone-Yumoto platform photograph: use its timber-clad covered
   platform, arched canopy, tactile paving, stairs, and realistic traveler scale
   as factual form reference, not material to copy.
6. Official Hakone-Yumoto concourse photograph: use its open station volume and
   restrained wood/metal finishes as secondary factual reference.
7. Official baggage-counter photograph: use only to understand that baggage
   handling occurs inside the station; do not reproduce its people, lettering,
   posters, or counter graphics.

## Scene

Late morning inside the covered Hakone-Yumoto station, viewed along the
platform-concourse threshold. The timber and metal station structure, yellow
tactile paving, stairs or escalator, and a glimpse of the green valley must
make the location feel like the entrance to a mountain railway rather than a
generic city station. A restrained red-and-cream local mountain train may wait
in the middle distance, without logos or route lettering.

All four guides stand together in the lower-right third, at credible traveler
scale. Aya-chan and Lala Xia are clearly visible beside one medium rolling
suitcase and two small day bags. Aya keeps the day's small bag while Lala Xia
checks the suitcase handle; their body language shows a practical pause before
they separate the day luggage from the overnight luggage. Sasa-kun holds the
approved patchwork leather notebook open to a blank page. Zhuangzi robot looks
towards the two possible continuations through the station. The action should
feel observed and useful, not posed for a group portrait.

Use bright natural daylight and a vivid but credible palette: warm timber,
vermilion train accents, clear greens beyond the station, yellow tactile paving,
and neutral metal. Render the station realistically and the four guides as
tactile stop-motion figures integrated with correct shadows and stable footing.
The station and transfer environment must carry at least two-thirds of the
visual attention.

## Hard Constraints

- Exactly the four approved guides and no other people, duplicates, mascots, or
  substitute characters.
- Aya-chan and Lala Xia remain unmistakable at B6 size and in a 390 px crop.
- One rolling suitcase only; no impossible pile of luggage and no unattended
  bag blocking tactile paving.
- No readable text, lettering, logos, platform numbers, tickets, captions,
  watermarks, pseudo-Japanese signs, or invented station graphics.
- Do not place a baggage counter on the platform, combine Odawara architecture
  with Hakone-Yumoto, or show a Shinkansen in the mountain station.
- No unsafe track access, sitting on tactile paving, or robot standing at an
  implausible size.
- Horizontal 3:2 composition at the largest available resolution, with safe
  crops for a landscape B6 figure page and a 16:9 website image.
