# Hakone Chapter 2 Odawara Transfer Figure Brief

Create a high-resolution horizontal travel-editorial scene for Chapter 2 of a
B6 Hakone guide. The visual job is to make Odawara feel like a deliberate
interchange between an intercity railway and the local line into Hakone, not a
generic station or an attraction stop.

## Reference Order

1. Aya-chan: preserve the red-panda face, navy sailor outfit, red scarf, brown
   shoes, and small brown case.
2. Lala Xia: preserve the panda form and cream overalls.
3. Sasa-kun: preserve the child in the black-and-white panda hooded outfit.
4. Zhuangzi robot: preserve the white articulated humanoid body and camera-like
   head; omit chest lettering rather than inventing it.
5. Official Odawara local-line platform photograph: use the broad glazed roof,
   platform spacing, stairs, and railway scale as factual form reference.
6. Official Odakyu/Hakone Tozan gate photograph: use the open steel-and-glass
   concourse and current gate arrangement as structural reference only.
7. Official Shinkansen gate photograph: use it only to understand the change
   between systems; do not copy its signs, posters, or lettering.

## Scene

Show a bright, spacious interior passage at Odawara Station where a traveler
has just left the Shinkansen side and is moving upward towards the shared
Odakyu/Hakone Tozan side. Use a credible modern Japanese station with a high
steel-and-glass roof, a visible vertical change by broad stairs or escalator,
yellow tactile paving, and glimpses of local railway platforms. The two systems
should be understood through space and movement, not through labels.

All four guides move together through the middle-right of the frame at
credible traveler scale. Aya-chan leads with the small brown case, Lala Xia
pulls one medium suitcase, Sasa-kun checks the approved patchwork leather
notebook, and Zhuangzi robot looks up towards the next level. Their movement
should look purposeful and candid, not like a posed group portrait. Keep the
station and vertical transfer as at least two-thirds of the visual attention.

Use clear daylight and a vivid but realistic palette: cool silver structure,
clean blue glass, a warm coral route accent, yellow tactile paving, and small
green glimpses outside. Render the architecture realistically and the four
guides as tactile stop-motion figures integrated with correct shadows and
stable footing.

## Hard Constraints

- Exactly the four approved guides; no anonymous passengers, staff, silhouettes,
  reflections, duplicates, mascots, or substitute characters.
- Aya-chan and Lala Xia remain unmistakable at B6 size and in a 390 px crop.
- One rolling suitcase only; no bag blocks tactile paving or a gate.
- No readable text, lettering, logos, platform numbers, tickets, captions,
  watermarks, pseudo-Japanese signs, posters, or route maps.
- Do not combine Odawara with Hakone-Yumoto timber architecture, place a
  Shinkansen on a local mountain platform, or show Odawara Castle inside the
  station.
- No unsafe track access, impossible floor connections, or robot at an
  implausible scale.
- Horizontal 3:2 composition at the largest available resolution, with safe
  crops for a landscape B6 figure page and a 16:9 website image.
