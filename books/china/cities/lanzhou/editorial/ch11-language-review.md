# Chapter 11 Language Review

Status: Chinese, Japanese, and English editorial text accepted on `2026-08-23`
after independent prose, compiled B6, and `390 px` responsive reading passes.

## Shared Route

All ten blocks answer one question: can one nearby branch be completed from
the Lanzhou lodging and back with the outward trip, entrance, internal
movement, return, and cancellation rule understood? Bingling-Liujiaxia,
Xinglong, and Ink Danxia remain alternatives. The Yellow-Tao confluence is
subordinate to a separately confirmed Liujiaxia road plan, never a fourth
route or an assumed boat stop.

History remains attached to what the traveler can inspect. Cave 169's `420 CE`
inscription anchors one documented stage in Bingling's long development; it
does not turn the complex into a one-date origin story. Food, weather,
mobility, and current operations appear only where they protect the complete
return.

## Chinese

The Chinese pass uses direct route language such as `完整往返`, `只选一条`,
`去程`, `回程`, `折返时刻`, and `整条取消`. It distinguishes arriving at
`刘家峡` from reaching `炳灵寺`, and keeps `黄洮交汇` inside a conditional
road decision. `建弘元年`, `西秦`, and `420年` explain how to read one cave
without claiming that every part of the site belongs to the same period.

Operational language is deliberately dated. `兴隆山` remains outside the
ready-to-follow plan until the entrance, S104 approach, visitor route,
ascent/descent, and closing time are confirmed directly. `水墨丹霞` is read
through bands, gullies, depth, and a managed path rather than promotional
color claims.

## Japanese

The Japanese pass is organized around ordinary travel decisions: `往路`,
`入口`, `園内移動`, `復路`, `引き返す時刻`, and `枝ごと外す`. It keeps
`炳霊寺`, `劉家峡`, `黄河・洮河合流点`, `興隆山`, and `蘭州水墨丹霞`
distinct. The Bingling history paragraph uses `建弘元年` and `西秦` as a
viewing anchor, not as a detached chronology.

Literal or administrative phrasing was removed during the independent pass.
The final text tells the reader what to confirm, what to observe, and when to
return. The Xinglong paragraph states the evidence gap plainly and does not
infer reopening from an old listing or a photograph.

## English

The English pass consistently uses `complete return`, `one branch`, `same-day
check`, and `turn-back time`. It does not rank the three places, invent a grand
loop, or promise a water-road connection. The confluence paragraph explains
why river color varies; the Danxia paragraph asks the reader to observe form
and depth without expecting a saturated photograph.

Awkward shorthand and abstract phrasing were removed in the final pass. Each
paragraph begins with a traveler action or place, follows one causal line, and
ends with a usable decision. No reader-facing sentence discusses image
generation or technical production.

## Alignment Result

The three languages align by route decision, evidence, and consequence rather
than sentence shape. Durable history and landscape interpretation remain
separate from volatile opening, road, vessel, ticket, shuttle, weather, and
accessibility claims. The prose is closed unless a concrete factual, language,
reading, layout, or parity failure is demonstrated.
