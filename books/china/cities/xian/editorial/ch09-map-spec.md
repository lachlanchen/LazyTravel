# Chapter 9 Map Spec: Stay Areas

## Reader Question

Which side of Xi'an should hold the first full morning and the final night?

## Required Geography

- inside the Ming wall, anchored at the Bell Tower;
- Yongning Gate / South Gate;
- Big Wild Goose Pagoda as the Yanta/Qujiang anchor;
- Xi'an North railway station;
- Lintong, represented as the declared midpoint between Huaqing Palace and the
  Qin museum used by the reviewed Chapter 7 choice map;
- a generalized Ming-wall rectangle and selected orientation links only.

## Layout

- landscape master suitable for the B6 landscape map page;
- one broad regional panel for Xi'an North, the city core, and Lintong;
- one enlarged central inset separating inside-wall, South Gate, and
  Yanta/Qujiang labels;
- five numbered zone markers shared by the map and decision cards;
- five concise trilingual cards stating the itinerary fit and the main check;
- minimum effective B6 text size of approximately 7 pt for map labels and 6 pt
  for card text;
- vivid coral, cobalt, jade, berry, and saffron accents on a clean white field.

## Explicit Limits

- not a complete metro map;
- not a hotel inventory or endorsement map;
- no prices, star ratings, room counts, review scores, or availability;
- no walking, taxi, or rail times;
- no claim that a zone boundary is administrative or exact;
- no line may imply a live route, guaranteed transfer, or straight-line walk;
- current operations must be rechecked at booking and before arrival.

## QA Gates

- exactly five zones in the locked order;
- no overlap among numbered markers, trilingual labels, inset, or cards;
- the three central zones remain distinct at B6 landscape size;
- the five cards remain readable at 390 px mobile width with the site's map
  zoom control;
- SVG text remains selectable and all fonts are embedded in the PDF;
- PNG master is at least 5,000 px wide;
- print and mobile proofs are visually inspected before approval.
