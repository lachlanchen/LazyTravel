# Xi'an Before-Departure Four-Guide Figure

Use case: `photorealistic-natural`

Asset type: landscape editorial figure for a B6 multilingual travel guide and
its responsive website.

Primary request: Create a realistic, warm evening travel-preparation scene
using the seven supplied continuity references. Aya-chan, Lala Xia, Sasa-kun,
and the Zhuangzi robot are four equal guide friends, all present exactly once
and all clearly readable at pocket-book size. Preserve their established head
shapes, faces, proportions, clothing cues, and the robot's white-and-black
mechanical construction.

Scene/backdrop: A quiet, contemporary Xi'an hotel room beside the city wall.
The four guides gather around a modest wooden table on the evening before a
full travel day. Through a broad window, show a restrained, credible view of a
lit Xi'an wall segment and one gate tower at middle distance, with ordinary
modern city lights beyond. The wall establishes place but does not become a
fantasy palace or historical reconstruction.

Action and objects: Two guides compare a generic identity-size card with a
booking confirmation on a phone; one checks a small route card; the robot has
the open patchwork leather notebook turned to a simple blank route diagram.
Place the folded LightMind glasses and the compact word-card device naturally
near the notebook. These are quiet working tools, not product shots. Keep all
card and screen content abstract and unreadable.

Style/medium: Photorealistic editorial photograph of tactile handcrafted
characters physically present in a real room. Natural material detail,
credible contact shadows, realistic wood, cloth, leather, metal, and glass.
Not a cartoon frame, not glossy toy advertising, not a children's poster.

Composition/framing: Horizontal 4:3 image. Table and four guides fill the lower
two-thirds; city-wall context stays visible in the upper third. Use a slightly
elevated three-quarter view so every face, both hands where practical, the
notebook, one blank card, and the phone can be understood without crowding.
Keep a clear margin around the group for B6 cropping. No extra people or
duplicate characters.

Lighting/mood: Warm practical lamp light on the group, balanced with clean blue
evening light outside. Calm concentration and companionable preparation. Vivid
vermilion, teal, cobalt, leaf green, and warm neutral accents without a yellow
cast, orange wash, dark-blue monotone, or purple gradient.

Text: none.

Constraints: Exactly four guide characters. Aya-chan and Lala Xia must be
immediately recognizable. Sasa-kun remains one human guide in the established
hooded outfit. The Zhuangzi robot is a full guide friend, not furniture or a
small mascot. Show one notebook, one phone, one small route/word card, and one
pair of folded glasses. The wall remains present-day architecture. Preserve
anatomy and object scale. The output must work as a figure, not a cover.

Avoid: readable letters, numbers, logos, QR codes, watermarks, captions,
interface text, fake Chinese characters, maps with labels, passports with real
data, additional animals, additional robots, extra limbs, fused hands, cropped
faces, food styling, luggage piles, tourism-ad poses, spectacle, fantasy
lantern streets, historical costumes, and a dominant golden-yellow palette.
