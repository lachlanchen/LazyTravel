# Chapter 1 Fact Review

Status: reviewed on `2026-08-16`.

## Chapter Job

The chapter explains one practical fact before the itinerary begins: Hakone is
small on a map but not flat, continuous, or operationally uniform. Terrain,
transfers, volcanic restrictions, and weather determine how much of the route
can be used in one day. History appears only where it explains the landform now
crossed by the traveler.

## Factual Boundaries

- The map's colored links describe transfer structure, not surveyed rail, road,
  ropeway, cable, bus, or cruise alignments.
- GSI terrain heights are samples rounded to ten metres; they are not surveyed
  platform elevations.
- `724 m` is presented as the municipality's approximate Lake Ashi elevation,
  not a claim that every shore point shares one exact height.
- The chapter does not publish fares, last departures, closures, alert levels,
  or a current trail state. It sends the reader to the operator, JMA, and Hakone
  Town on the morning of travel.
- Mount Fuji is not shown in the chapter figure or promised in the prose.
- The representative Lake Ashi scene does not claim one exact camera position.

## Editorial Result

The geological sequence supports the route rather than becoming a separate
history essay. No dynasty, shrine tradition, food claim, hotel recommendation,
or attraction ranking is pulled forward from a later chapter. This keeps the
opening chapter coherent and avoids using unrelated facts merely to make it
sound comprehensive.
