# Xi'an North Interchange Figure Prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural

Asset type: 3:2 landscape editorial figure for a multilingual B6 travel guide
and responsive website.

Primary request: Create a high-resolution realistic travel scene at the
forecourt threshold of Xi'an North railway station. Use the official station
image only as factual architectural and spatial reference for the broad low
roof, long glazed entrance, rail-hub scale, and surrounding modern city.
Depict a clear late-afternoon arrival rather than copying its night photograph.
Include exactly the four recurring LazyTravel guide travelers from the four
private identity references: Aya-chan, Lala Xia, Sasa-kun, and the white-and-
black Zhuangzi robot. They travel as four equal buddies.

Scene/backdrop: Xi'an North's station facade and broad organized forecourt
dominate at least the upper two thirds. The four guides stand together on a
wide unobstructed pedestrian area near the station threshold with compact
realistic rolling luggage and one small day bag. They have paused before
choosing metro, taxi, or ride-hailing rather than rushing toward the first
escalator.

Style/medium: polished realistic editorial travel photography with physically
believable architecture, paving, daylight, materials, shadows, and depth. The
characters retain their high-quality tactile 3D reference appearance and are
integrated naturally at travel-guide scale.

Composition/framing: 3:2 horizontal, station centered and fully legible as a
place, four guides in the lower quarter in a loose group, all four unobstructed
and visually distinct. Show Aya-chan and Lala Xia in readable three-quarter
view, with Sasa-kun and the robot equally clear. Leave safe crop margins for
print.

Lighting/mood: vivid clean late-afternoon light, cobalt sky, warm but natural
highlights, crisp visibility, calm purposeful arrival.

Color palette: vivid vermilion, jade, cobalt, and coral accents against neutral
stone and glass; balanced natural color and no yellow cast.

Constraints: exactly four guide characters. Preserve Aya-chan's navy sailor
outfit and red scarf, Lala Xia's light overalls, Sasa-kun's black-and-white
hooded outfit and human face, and the Zhuangzi robot's articulated white-and-
black body. Compact luggage stays beside the group and does not block
circulation. The station and the transfer decision remain the subject.

Avoid: extra foreground characters, duplicated guides, changed identities,
giant characters, obscured faces, advertising poses, fantasy or historical
architecture, a crowded concourse, fake Chinese text, readable timetables,
platform numbers, fares, logos, brand marks, watermarks, captions, arrows, UI
overlays, blur, haze, dark lighting, yellow grading, cut-off feet, blocked
walkways, or unsafe behavior.
