# Lanzhou Railway Station Arrival Plate

Create a high-resolution, text-free, realistic editorial travel image for a B6
pocket guide. Landscape `16:9`, 4K.

## Destination And Composition

- Show the front of Lanzhou Railway Station as the eastern rail gate in clear
  daytime. Preserve the recognizable broad rectangular glass facade, central
  square clock, projecting entrance canopy, taller cylindrical corner building
  to the left, and dry mountain slope close behind the right side. The station
  must dominate the image and remain recognizable at B6 size.
- Use the supplied station photograph only to understand those durable visual
  relationships. Create a new, centred but slightly oblique street-level view;
  do not reproduce the source camera position or crowd.
- Include exactly four guide travelers and no other person, crowd, vendor,
  driver, staff member, face, silhouette, reflection-person, poster-person, or
  fifth traveler. A few unbranded vehicles may establish scale but remain
  secondary and have no readable plates.
- Final cleanup is strict: leave the entrance, pavement, road, and every window
  empty except for the four named guides. Remove all tiny background people,
  human-shaped reflections, advertisement figures, noticeboards, route panels,
  posters, roadside signs, buses, cars, and license plates. Keep only the
  architecture, clock, trees, railings, road surface, mountain, and four guides.
- Do not turn the station into Lanzhou West, an airport, a traditional palace,
  a high-speed rail megaterminal, or a clock tower detached from the facade. Do
  not invent snowy alpine peaks, historic city walls, or a railway train in the
  road forecourt.
- No raster text, station lettering, logos, train numbers, wayfinding text,
  watermarks, captions, map graphics, or symbols on clothing, luggage,
  notebook, or robot panel.

## Four Guide Continuity

Include exactly Aya-chan, Lala Xia, Sasa-kun, and the Zhuangzi robot, each
clearly based on the supplied identity reference. The robot is an equal friend
and traveler, never luggage or equipment. Preserve each guide's face, hair,
outfit language, proportions, and color cues.

three-quarter front view with every face and both eyes visible. Their luggage
is compact and plausible. Aya is the red-panda schoolgirl in a navy sailor
uniform; Lala Xia is the round panda in cream overalls; Sasa-kun is the human

The patchwork notebook may be closed in Aya-chan's hand. Do not force the
LightMind glasses or word-card device into the scene.

## Visual Character

Realistic editorial travel photography with tactile miniature guide figures
integrated into a credible urban railway-station forecourt. Natural glass,
concrete, clock face, mountain rock, trees, fabric, fur, skin, and articulated
robot surfaces. Clear daylight with vivid but credible white, vermilion, jade,
cobalt, and coral accents. No dull yellow cast, purple gradient, decorative
bokeh, poster typography, cinematic darkness, or toy-diorama blur.

## Factual Limits

The location reference establishes the station facade, clock, corner tower,
and nearby mountain relationship only. The new image is a recognition view,
not evidence for an entrance, metro route, pickup zone, current construction,
accessibility path, train service, crowd level, or exact camera position.
