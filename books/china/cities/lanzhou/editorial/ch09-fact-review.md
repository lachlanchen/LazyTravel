## Travel Question

The chapter answers one question: **how much of Lanzhou fits into the usable
part of one, two, or three days after bags, meals, reservations, weather, and
the way out are counted?**

The plans are nested, but a one-day visitor still chooses between two complete
days rather than attempting both:

- **River day:** one morning bowl, the south-bank approach, Zhongshan Bridge,
  and either White Pagoda Hill's lower terraces or the full climb.
- **Museum day:** one morning bowl, the booked Gansu Provincial Museum route,
  a seated meal, and only a modest eastward continuation.
- **Two days:** keep the museum and river days separate. Swap their order when
  the reservation, arrival gate, or weather requires it.
- **Three days:** keep the first two days intact. Use the third for one city
  height when visibility and the complete return are suitable, or for one slow
  old-city thread when they are not.

An arrival afternoon or departure morning is not silently counted as a full
day. A plan is usable only when it names the bag decision, principal anchor,
seated meal or rest, and a return that protects the next fixed commitment.

## Rejected Shortcuts

- “Lanzhou can be completed in one day.” A traveler can complete one coherent
  Lanzhou day; the city has no completion checklist.
- A one-day schedule containing the provincial museum, the full bridge-hill
  route, Lanshan, a night market, and every named food. It leaves no credible
  museum visit, meal, rest, or recovery from a delayed transfer.
- Treating an airport arrival afternoon as Day 1 without baggage reclaim and
  the actual city transfer. The first usable block begins only after bags and
  the route to the hotel are settled.
- Minute-by-minute timing assembled from vehicle estimates. Station interiors,
  waiting, security, bags, meals, toilets, slopes, and closures make false
  precision worse than a clear decision envelope.
- “Rainy day equals museum.” The museum may be closed, fully booked, or
  difficult to reach. Replace the whole anchor only after checking it.
- Moving the hill to the hottest, coldest, or latest part of the day merely to
  keep every other stop. Remove the hill when a safe and comfortable complete
  ascent and descent no longer fits.
- Completing White Pagoda Hill, Lanshan, and Wuquan in one trip as proof of
  seeing Lanzhou. One useful height is enough for this itinerary.
- Turning missing visibility into a promise that the skyline will improve
  later. Use the ground-level city day without apology.
- Calling queues, transfers, or standing in a station “rest.” A usable day
  includes a place to sit, eat, drink, and use a toilet.

## Locked Block Sequence

1. **Figure:** count the usable day in the river valley; write bags, anchor,
   meal, and exit before adding a stop.
2. **Map:** read nested one-, two-, and three-day lanes and the whole-day swap
   rule.
3. **Figure:** one-day river plan with one crossing and lower terraces or the
   full White Pagoda Hill climb.
4. **Figure:** one-day museum plan with a booked entry, the accepted three-part
   route, a seated meal, and no compulsory hill.
5. **Figure:** two-day Day 1 moves west to centre: breakfast, museum, lunch,
   then one modest old-city continuation.
6. **Figure:** two-day Day 2 crosses once and uses White Pagoda Hill as the one
   height; lower terraces replace the full climb when needed.
7. **Figure:** three-day Day 3 selects Lanshan only when visibility, ascent,
   descent, and return have all been checked.
8. **Figure:** the slower third-day alternative follows one old-city thread and
   one seated tea or meal instead of forcing a second hill.
9. **Practical:** swap complete days for museum closure, heat, cold, rain, poor
   visibility, or an unsafe hillside. Heavy rain means reliable shelter or
   rest; a ground-level walk is used only while city conditions remain safe.
10. **Callout:** put baggage, food, rest, and the usable exit on one clock, then
    cut optional items in a fixed order.

Exactly ten blocks are required. Fewer blocks would hide the two valid one-day
choices or collapse the three-day fallback into the hill plan. More blocks
would repeat the detailed attraction, food, lodging, and transport chapters.
