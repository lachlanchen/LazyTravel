# Hakone Checkpoint Reconstruction Figure Brief

Use case: photorealistic-natural

Create a high-resolution realistic editorial attraction image in a `3:2` landscape
composition. Show the present reconstructed Hakone Checkpoint on the Lake Ashi
shore from an oblique visitor approach closely grounded in the supplied official
photographs. The destination must occupy most of the frame: black-stained timber
palisades, the broad dark wooden gate with its low grey shingled roof, adjoining
single-storey guard buildings, pale compacted ground, and the enclosed slope behind.
Lake Ashi may appear only as a narrow blue-grey glimpse beyond the buildings. Do
not show Mount Fuji or any other snow mountain. Keep the architecture sober and
materially plausible. The reconstruction should read as a whole site controlling a
narrow passage, not as one generic temple gate.

Show exactly the four recurring LazyTravel guides, using all four supplied identity
references:

- Aya-chan, the red-panda girl in her established navy sailor outfit;
- Lala Xia, the taller panda companion in her established light travel outfit;
- Sasa-kun, the smaller companion in the established panda hood;
- Zhuangzi, the white articulated humanoid robot with a completely blank white
  chest panel: no logo, icon, mascot, lettering, or mark of any kind.

Stage all four at traveler scale near the lower edge, occupying less than one eighth
of the image. Aya-chan and Lala Xia pause before the gate and look through the
passage; Sasa-kun studies the palisade line; Zhuangzi looks towards the hillside
watch position. Preserve each face, outfit, body, and relative height. Keep all
four unobstructed and distinct, with ordinary contemporary travel posture rather
than a mascot pose. Use clear late-morning natural light, vivid lake cobalt, cedar
green, neutral grey, deep timber black, and a small coral accent; avoid dull yellow
or sepia grading.

Use the supplied official site photographs only for gate, palisade, building,
hillside, and lake relationships. Create a new composition rather than copying one
photograph. Do not place the guides inside an Edo-period event or imply that the
current buildings are untouched originals.

Prohibited: Mount Fuji, a snow mountain, or an impossible panoramic backdrop; a
freestanding open observation tower or fantasy watchtower; any fifth person;
duplicated, hybrid, or missing guides; anonymous staff or visitors; samurai,
guards, weapons, prisoners, inspection reenactment, cosplay, invented banners,
purple drapery, museum mannequins, temple ornaments, red shrine architecture,
fantasy castle scale, or modern ticket signage as a focal point; readable labels,
logos, chest emblems, brand marks, pseudo-Japanese text, or watermarks; anime,
storybook, painterly, mascot-poster, or cinematic-fantasy styling.
