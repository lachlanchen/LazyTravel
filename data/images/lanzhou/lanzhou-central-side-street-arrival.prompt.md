# Lanzhou Central Side-street Arrival Plate

Create a high-resolution, horizontal, realistic editorial travel image for a
B6 pocket guide. Show a believable central Lanzhou street at early evening:
one lively commercial street with restrained shop and food-stall light remains
visible in the middle distance, but the street and stalls are momentarily empty
of customers and workers. A quieter, well-lit side-street hotel entrance is
clear in the foreground. The scene must explain a lodging decision: central is
convenient for dinner and the old-city route, but the exact entrance and the
quiet side of the building still matter. Use dry northwestern-city materials,
ordinary mid-rise buildings, clean paving, realistic street lighting, and a
small glimpse of the close valley hills. Do not reproduce or name a real hotel.

Include exactly the four recurring travel-guide characters shown in the four
supplied references, each once and clearly distinct. Aya-chan is the red-panda
mascot in a navy sailor outfit with a small brown satchel. Lala Xia is the round
panda mascot in cream overalls. Sasa-kun is the boy in a black-and-white panda
hoodie. The Zhuangzi robot is the tall white humanoid friend with black joints
and a camera-like head. Match the references closely; Aya-chan and Lala Xia are
mascots, not humans. Put the group together near the side-street entrance with
light overnight luggage, looking once toward the lively street and once toward
the entrance. They provide traveler scale and remain secondary to the street.

The four guides are the only people or character-like figures anywhere in the
image. No distant pedestrian, vendor, customer, child, silhouette, reflected
person, mannequin, hotel clerk, driver, duplicated character, readable sign,
brand, caption, border, or watermark. Do not imply a river view, spotless
room, guaranteed quiet, luxury, step-free access, or a specific operating
property. Use a clean 16:9 composition with vivid but natural vermilion, jade,
cobalt, and coral accents. Avoid yellow wash, sepia, heavy fog, neon fantasy,
oversized characters, and generic international-city architecture. Leave safe
edge space for the print crop.

If a rejected draft is supplied as the first reference, preserve its useful
empty-street, side-entrance composition and the four guide identities, but
clean it rigorously: remove every distant figure and vehicle occupant; replace
all shop, door, wall, robot, and luggage lettering or logos with plain
unlettered color panels or natural materials. The final image contains exactly
four guides and no legible or pseudo-legible text anywhere.
