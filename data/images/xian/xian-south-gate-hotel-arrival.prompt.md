# Xi'an South Gate Hotel Arrival Figure

Use case: `photorealistic-natural`

Asset type: full-bleed landscape editorial figure for a B6 multilingual travel
guide and its responsive website.

Primary request: Create a realistic evening travel scene in Xi'an in which the
four recurring guide friends pause at the correct entrance of a contemporary,
unbranded hotel near Yongning Gate. Aya-chan and Lala Xia must be immediately
recognizable from their references; Sasa-kun and the Zhuangzi robot are equal
travel companions, not props. The action is practical and calm: compact
luggage stays beside the group, Aya checks the entrance, Lala checks the
booking, and the other two look toward the doorway before
everyone enters.

Input images:

- Aya-chan reference: preserve red-panda identity, face, scale, and clothing
  continuity.
- Lala Xia reference: preserve panda identity, face, scale, and clothing
  continuity.
- Sasa-kun reference: preserve panda-hood identity, face, scale, and clothing
  continuity.
- Zhuangzi robot reference: preserve the exact friendly robot identity and
  treat the robot as the fourth person.
- Yongning Gate reference: use only for the recognizable gate, wall mass,
  masonry, and spatial relationship, not as a source image to reproduce.

Scene/backdrop: The hotel entrance occupies one side of the frame with warm
interior light, a visible but unbranded canopy, level threshold, glass doors,
and a clear pedestrian path. Across the street and behind the group, Yongning
Gate and the long grey-brick city wall are plainly visible and remain the
dominant Xi'an place signal. Use plausible street trees, paving, restrained
traffic, and natural summer-evening city light.

Style/medium: high-resolution realistic editorial travel photography with
physically plausible lighting, materials, perspective, and character scale;
not a poster, cartoon panel, game render, collage, or travel advertisement.

Composition/framing: wide 4:3 landscape, eye-level, all four friends shown
clearly from head to at least knee, Aya and Lala unobstructed, doorway and
Yongning Gate both legible as spatial anchors, compact luggage grouped safely
beside them, open circulation path, no one centered like a mascot lineup.

Lighting/mood: vivid but natural blue-hour light, warm hotel doorway, accurate
skin/fur/material texture, calm arrival after a day of travel. Keep shadow
detail and avoid orange/yellow color cast.

Color palette: clear cobalt evening sky, grey city-wall brick, green street
trees, warm but restrained interior light, and the guides' established colors.

Constraints: show exactly the four recurring friends; include Aya and Lala;
preserve identities and relative scale; keep Xi'an architecture dominant;
luggage must not block the door or pavement; no hotel
brand, no fictional heritage facade, no historic costume, no extra guide
characters.

Avoid: readable generated signs, Chinese text, room numbers, prices, stars,
booking-platform UI, QR codes, logos, watermark, promotional pose, exaggerated
luxury, empty atmospheric street, blurry wall, incorrect gate silhouette,
giant robot, duplicate characters, cropped faces, distorted hands or luggage,
golden monochrome, or cinematic haze.
