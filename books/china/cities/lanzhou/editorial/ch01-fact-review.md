# Chapter 1 Fact Review

Status: accepted on `2026-08-21`.

## Chapter Job

The opening chapter teaches one practical fact before any itinerary begins:
Lanzhou's first-visit core is a long east-west river valley, not a compact
circle around one center. The reader should finish the chapter able to identify
east and west, north and south bank, a necessary river crossing, and the
arrival gate closest to the next stop.

History, food, hotel names, attraction rankings, exact journey times, and
bridge construction details stay in later chapters. This chapter uses only the
geography and current transport structure needed to avoid a poor first route.

## Factual Boundaries

- The `40+ km` and `about 1 km` figures are quoted only as the cited official
  source's description of main-city morphology. They do not define the full
  municipality or a walkable visitor district.
- The map's hill bands explain orientation. They are not surveyed contours,
  slope grades, park boundaries, or a claim of step-free access.
- The Yellow River geometry is clipped from OpenStreetMap way `166825103` at a
  pinned snapshot. It is for overview, not navigation or flood conditions.
- Point records use pinned OpenStreetMap objects and official operator station
  names. A marker identifies a useful anchor, not an exact entrance or
  platform.
- Metro links are schematic. The chapter publishes no fare, first or last
  train, exit number, travel time, crowd level, or temporary service state.
- The airport inset shows direction and separation from the central map. It is
  not drawn to the same scale and does not publish a transfer time.
- The generated panorama is a representative orientation scene based on
  documented location references. It does not promise a particular river
  color, skyline clarity, boat, season, crowd level, or viewpoint access.

## Editorial Result Required

The chapter passes only if a reader can use the map without reading a general
history of Gansu. The last block must lead directly to the arrival-gate
decision in Chapter 2. No summary paragraph is added merely to make the chapter
sound complete.
