## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.

Create a high-resolution horizontal 3:2 editorial travel image on top of the
present Xi'an City Wall in clear daytime. The attraction must dominate: a broad
grey-brick rampart paved for visitors, low crenellated parapets receding in
perspective, and one believable two-storey gate tower in the middle distance.
Show enough modern Xi'an roofline beyond the wall to make clear that this is a
historic structure inside a living city. The image should help a reader judge
the wall's width and the walking distance ahead; it is a representative view,
not a claim to one exact gate or access point.

Place exactly two recurring guides, Aya-chan and Lala Xia, walking side by side
in the lower foreground. Preserve both supplied identities precisely, with
coherent full bodies, footwear, ground contact, natural shadows, and
recognizable three-quarter faces. They occupy no more than roughly one fifth
of the image height and leave most of the rampart unobstructed. No props are
needed.

Style: realistic documentary travel photography with tactile character guides,
crisp grey masonry, natural sky and trees, vivid but balanced vermilion, jade,
cobalt, and coral accents; no yellow cast, heavy fog, or dark grading.

Constraints: exactly two guides and no anonymous visitors; believable masonry,
parapets, drainage and tower proportions; no Tang-period reconstruction, army,
ceremony, lantern festival, staged costume, bicycles, rental sign, readable
text, logo, caption, watermark, traffic floating on the wall, duplicate tower,
duplicate character, malformed paws, clipped ears, or cut-off feet.
