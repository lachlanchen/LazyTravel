## Chapter Question

The chapter answers one question: **how should a traveler use the southbound
Lake Ashi crossing when the weather or service changes?** Togendai is the
decision point. Moto-Hakone suits a shrine-first day; Hakone-machi suits an
Onshi Park or old-road continuation. The lake, shrine, wakasagi, and former
imperial-villa site form one geographic sequence. History explains those
places and never becomes a detached survey.

## Rejected Shortcuts

- "Board the first boat at Togendai." Rejected. The port, wind, next service,
  lodging direction, and desired first stop decide the route.
- "Rain cancels the cruise." Rejected. The operator distinguishes ordinary
  rain from fog, strong wind, and unsafe conditions.
- "Lake Ashi means a guaranteed Fuji view." Rejected. Shoreline, ridges,
  weather, and route remain useful when Fuji is hidden.
- "Hakone Shrine was certainly founded in 757." Rejected without attribution.
  The date belongs to the shrine's engi tradition.
- "The lakeside torii is ancient." Rejected. It dates to 1952, with the plaque
  added in 1964.
- "Wakasagi is available fresh all year." Rejected. Fresh catch is seasonal;
  processed forms are the reliable year-round option.
- A generic soba, curry, or cafe stop presented as Lake Ashi food. Rejected.
  Wakasagi provides the defensible place-food link in this chapter.
- Generated figures as exact documentary camera records. Rejected. They show
  place form, route logic, visitor scale, and food form without claiming one
  exact moment.

## Block Spine

1. Reach Togendai and choose the destination port before joining a queue.
2. Read one map with boat, bus fallback, shrine, food, park, and exit choices.
3. Cross the lake as transport and as a way to understand its ridges and weather.
4. Distinguish ordinary rain from conditions that can change or stop the boat.
5. Walk from Moto-Hakone into the shrine forest and keep tradition, period, and visible place aligned.
6. Worship before treating the modern Heiwa torii as an optional photograph.
7. Eat Lake Ashi wakasagi in a preparation that suits the time and season.
8. Verify catch, restaurant participation, and stock instead of relying on an old listing.
9. Use Onshi Park to connect lake scenery with the former villa site.
10. Finish at Hakone-machi with enough margin for lodging or Chapter 6's old-road route.
