# Chapter 7 Language Review

Status: Chinese, Japanese, and English accepted on `2026-08-22`; compiled B6
and `390 px` responsive reading review pass.

## Shared Route

All ten blocks answer one question: which single height adds the clearest value
to the day already planned? White Pagoda Hill joins the bridge and two banks,
Lanshan explains the city's east-west valley form, and Wuquan Mountain offers a
layered park-and-heritage walk. The chapter never turns the three choices into
a ranking or a checklist.

History is attached to what the traveler can read on site. Santai Pavilion's
rebuilding history corrects the impression of an unchanged Ming structure;
Wuquan's layered construction and conservation status explain why current
barriers and closures set the route. Dated access examples are labelled as
dated evidence rather than current promises.

## Chinese

The Chinese pass leads with route verbs and visible checks: `接在中山桥之后`,
`沿黄河读城市的东西走向`, `是单程还是往返`, and `只选一条开放支线`.
The final pass removed abstract phrasing such as `提醒人们`, replaced `云底`
with conditions visible at the entrance, and separated pavilion rebuilding
from the later greening of the hill.

## Japanese

The Japanese pass was edited independently around normal travel decisions:
`下段でも未完ではない`, `上りと下りを一つの手配として決める`, and
`公開中の一画` are not literal mirrors of the Chinese. The final pass removed
the unnatural `階段の濡れ方`, avoided using the Chinese noun `院落` as ordinary
Japanese, and made low cloud, moving treetops, wet steps, and local
announcements concrete entrance checks.

## English

The English pass keeps instructions executable and avoids scenic sales copy.
It distinguishes a confirmed high point from a guaranteed viewpoint, a dated
holiday bus notice from year-round service, and protected heritage status from
today's doorway access. The final pass removed a clumsy
`courtyard-and-spring segment`, simplified the ascent/return check, and replaced
technical `cloud base` language with observable conditions.

## Alignment Result

The three languages align by choice, place, historical correction, live check,
turnaround, and stop rule rather than sentence shape. None promises a skyline,
cableway, bus, open through-route, drinkable spring, step-free path, or second
hill after descent. Page-level B6 and responsive-website review found no
language, ruby, clipping, or final-size legibility failure. The prose is closed
unless a later factual or rendering defect is demonstrated.
