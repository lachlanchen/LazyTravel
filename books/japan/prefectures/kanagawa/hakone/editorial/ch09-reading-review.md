## English Pass

The English pass removes translated constructions such as an interchange that
"does useful work" and a property one can "depart from more simply." The
final prose describes the exact gain or cost: reduced backtracking, a shorter
evening journey, written booking answers, a poor-weather exit, and a shorter
first leg next morning. `Representative room` remains an explicit illustration
limit, not a property recommendation or accessibility claim.
