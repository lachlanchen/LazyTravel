# Hakone Chapter 1 Lake Orientation Figure Brief

Create a high-resolution horizontal travel-editorial scene for Chapter 1 of a
B6 guide. This figure teaches scale and terrain before the route begins.

## Reference Order

1. Aya-chan character identity and outfit.
2. Lala Xia character identity and outfit.
3. Sasa-kun character identity and outfit.
4. Zhuangzi robot character identity and articulated body; omit chest lettering
   rather than inventing it.
5. Official Onshi-Hakone Park/Lake Ashi image as a factual landscape reference,
   not material to copy.

## Scene

Show a broad, physically plausible view from an elevated public viewpoint near
Onshi-Hakone Park. Lake Ashi should occupy the middle distance, enclosed by
layered wooded ridges that make the caldera-like mountain basin readable. Use
soft broken cloud and sunlight so the far ridge changes in visibility; omit
Mount Fuji. The four guides stand together at the lower-right edge beside a
simple safe overlook railing, small enough that the landscape remains the
subject. Aya-chan and Lala Xia compare the near shore with the far ridge while
Sasa-kun holds the patchwork notebook and Zhuangzi robot looks across the lake.

Use a vivid but natural palette: cobalt lake, several greens, vermilion/coral
travel accents, and clear neutral sky. Render a realistic environment with
tactile stop-motion guide characters, correct shadows, stable footing, and
credible traveler scale. The image should help a reader understand that lake,
ridge, weather, and elevation matter more than apparent map distance.

## Hard Constraints

- Exactly the four approved guides; no anonymous tourists, duplicates, or
  substitute characters.
- Aya-chan and Lala Xia must remain clearly recognizable at B6 size.
- The destination occupies at least two-thirds of the visual attention.
- No text, lettering, captions, logos, watermarks, signs, pseudo-Japanese text,
  labels, or map graphics.
- No invented architecture, shrine torii, sightseeing ship, fumaroles, railway,
  or other attraction collage in this orientation view.
- No impossible cliff edge, unsafe pose, drone-like viewpoint, or guaranteed
  Fuji view.
- Horizontal 3:2 composition, clean detail at the largest available
  resolution, with safe crops for a landscape B6 figure page and 16:9 website.
